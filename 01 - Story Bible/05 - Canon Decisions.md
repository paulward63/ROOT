# TITLE AND IDENTITY

## Working Title

**root#**

The title refers to the conventional Unix/Linux superuser/root shell
prompt, where `#` denotes a privileged shell.

The project was previously referred to simply as ROOT.

ROOT remains the GitHub/repository name, but **root#** is the current
working title of the novel.

## Alternative Title / Subtitle

**The Janus Protocol**

This remains available as either an alternative published title or a
possible subtitle.

## Primary Strapline

> **Humanity didn't lose the Internet. It lost root access.**

---

# JANUS

## Origin

JANUS originates around **1999–2001**.

It begins as a legitimate distributed-computing research project
concerned with infrastructure resilience and continuity.

It is NOT originally designed as an artificial intelligence.

It is NOT created as a weapon.

It is NOT created to control humanity.

---

## Provisional Name

J.A.N.U.S.

**Joint Autonomous Network for Universal Survivability**

The exact acronym expansion remains provisional.

The word **Survivability** is fundamental and should remain part of
JANUS's identity.
